Simple, clear and transparent information about shopping with YUGA.

Important: Please read these policies before placing an order with YUGA Fashion. By placing an order, you agree to the applicable terms mentioned below.

01. PRIVACY POLICY

YUGA Fashion respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you share with us.

Information We May Collect

When you contact us or place an order, we may receive information such as your name, phone number, delivery address and order details.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and confirm your orders.
  • To communicate with you regarding your order.
  • To arrange delivery of your purchase.
  • To provide customer support.

We do not intentionally sell your personal information to third parties.

We may share necessary information with delivery or service providers when required to complete your order.

02. TERMS & CONDITIONS

By using the YUGA Fashion website or placing an order with us, you agree to these terms.

Products

Product images are provided for representation. Actual colours may vary slightly depending on lighting, photography and your device display.

Product Availability

Product availability and sizes may change without prior notice. An item shown on the website is not guaranteed to remain available until an order has been confirmed.

Pricing

YUGA Fashion reserves the right to change product prices or offers when necessary. The price confirmed at the time of purchase will apply to that order.

Website Information

We aim to keep all website information accurate and updated. However, occasional errors in product information, pricing or availability may occur.

03. SHIPPING POLICY

Orders are processed after the order has been confirmed by YUGA Fashion.

Processing

Processing time may vary depending on product availability and order volume.

Delivery

Delivery time depends on the customer's location and the courier or delivery service used.

Delays caused by courier services, weather, public holidays, unforeseen events or circumstances outside YUGA Fashion's control may occur.

Incorrect Address

Customers are responsible for providing a correct and complete delivery address and contact information.

04. RETURN & EXCHANGE POLICY

YUGA Fashion aims to provide products in good condition and according to the confirmed order.

Exchange Requests

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect product, contact YUGA Fashion as soon as possible after delivery.

The product may need to be unused, unworn and in its original condition for an exchange to be considered.

Size Exchanges

Size exchanges may be considered depending on product availability and the condition of the returned product.

Items Not Eligible

  • Products that have been worn or washed.
  • Products damaged after delivery due to customer handling.
  • Products altered or modified by the customer.
  • Products returned without prior communication with YUGA Fashion.

Approval of a return or exchange is subject to inspection and availability.

05. CANCELLATION POLICY

If you need to cancel an order, contact YUGA Fashion as soon as possible.

Cancellation requests may not be accepted once an order has already been processed, packed or dispatched.

If an order has already been dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible.

06. PAYMENT POLICY

Payment methods available for an order will be communicated or displayed during the ordering process.

Orders may be confirmed only after the required payment or order verification has been completed.

Customers should keep their payment confirmation or transaction details until the order has been successfully received.
